15 · DOWN: reduction, defeat, and suppression

Scaling back, saying no, and keeping things quiet.

ACCESS

Read the scene and notice the moves in bold. One of them, pare down, is never taught in this book. Before you read on, let the particle show you what it means.

A finance team in a budget review, four percent over plan before the board meeting.

Farid We're four percent over plan. We need to cut down somewhere before the board review.

Ana We could scale down the regional expansion instead of killing it.

Farid Fine, but tone down the growth language in the deck. The board hates surprises.

Ana Marketing already turned down our request to trim their spend. They won't back down easily.

Farid Then we pare down travel and events first, and hold down headcount until the fourth quarter.

Ana Agreed. But let's not play down the risk. If revenue slips, none of this is enough.

ANALYSIS

Down is the mirror of up, and its hub is just as physical: toward the ground. Put a box down. Sit down. Every abstract sense in this unit is that lowering applied to a quantity, a position, or a signal.

In the reduction sense, a quantity is brought lower: cut down the budget, tone down the language. In the defeat sense, a position is brought down and ends: shut down a project, turn down a request. In the suppression sense, a signal is pushed down and held there: play down a risk, hold down costs.

The hub is the literal sense. Each spoke is a step away from it. This unit takes the three solid spokes; Unit 16 takes the rest.

Watch out. Turn down has two senses: reject a request, or lower the volume. And back down is not back off: back down means abandon a position you argued for; back off means stop pressing or pull away.
American / British English. Both varieties use scale down and scale back for reducing scope. Scale back is a little more common in the US, scale down in the UK. This book uses scale down, with scale back as an equal alternative.

Hold down means toward the ground, lower, and the only question is what gets lowered: a quantity, a position, or a signal you want quieter. This is why scale down sounds survivable and shut down sounds final, though they share a particle.
