English Communication for Professionals

Thirty-six units move through the verb system, modality, emphasis, cohesion, reporting, and register, ending in the reformulation and lexical control that mark real fluency, built for the whole professional world, not only executives.

At C1 the grammar is mostly there; what's missing is control. This book is about the choices that carry weight: who a sentence blames, how certain you sound, what lands first and last. Accuracy becomes strategy.

  • Control tone, stance, and emphasis on purpose
  • Concede ground without losing your position
  • Foreground the one thing that matters
  • Report and attribute with precision
