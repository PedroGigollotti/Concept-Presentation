Professional English Practice · Phrasal Verbs

16 · DOWN: recording, fixing, and settling

Getting it on paper, nailing it in place, and coming to rest.

ACCESS

Read the scene and notice the moves in bold. One of them, hunker down, is never taught in this book. Before you read on, let the particle show you what it means.

A hotel events team, eighteen hours before a large conference opens its doors.

Rhea Doors open in eighteen hours. Let's nail down the run of show before anything else.

Marco Agreed. I'll note down the final timings and take down any changes the vendors call in.

Rhea Good. Pin down the keynote's arrival time. We still don't have a firm one.

Marco The AV vendor keeps changing the setup. We lock down the stage plan tonight, no more edits.

Rhea Once that's set, the team can hunker down and run the full rehearsal.

Marco And after doors close, we wind down slowly. No teardown until the last guest leaves.

ANALYSIS

Down is the same hub you met in Unit 15: toward the ground. This one takes the senses where down means fixed to the ground: written onto the page, pinned in place, or come to rest.

In the recording sense, a fleeting thing is fixed onto paper: write it down, note it down. In the fixing sense, a loose thing is fastened so it cannot move: nail down the details, pin down a time. In the settling sense, motion slows and comes to rest: settle down, calm down, wind down.

The same wheel as Unit 15, turned. This unit takes the three solid spokes; the dotted ones were Unit 15.

Watch out. Take down has opposite-feeling senses: record a statement, dismantle a stage, or defeat someone, depending on the object. And pin down and nail down overlap but aren't identical: pin down leans toward a slippery person or vague thing; nail down leans toward details and plans.
American / British English. British English often drops the particle and just says note it. American English keeps note it down or jot it down. This book uses note down.

Hold “down means fixed to the ground,” and the only question is what gets fixed: a fleeting fact onto the page, a loose detail into place, or a racing team to a stop. This is why note it down feels light and lock it down feels final, though they share a particle.
