18 · OVER: transfer, review, excess, and concealment

Handing across, looking again, spilling past, and covering up.

ACCESS

Read the scene and notice the moves in bold. One of them, mull over, is never taught in this book. Before you read on, decide what the particle tells you it means.

A consulting team wrapping an engagement, the week before the client's staff take it on.

Dev The engagement's wrapping. We hand over the workstream to the client's team Friday.

Priya Before that, let's go over the findings once more. Two numbers still don't reconcile.

Dev Good. And the project ran over budget by nine percent. The client will ask about that.

Priya We don't gloss over it. We show exactly where the scope grew.

Dev Agreed. Let's mull over how to frame the overrun before the call.

Priya I'll talk it over with the client lead first, so nothing boils over in the room.

ANALYSIS

Over's hub is physical: across and above. Climb over a wall, spread a cloth over a table. Every abstract sense in this unit is that crossing or covering applied to control, attention, a limit, or a flaw.

In the transfer sense, control moves across from one hand to another: hand over, take over. In the review sense, attention passes back and forth: go over, talk over. In the excess sense, something crosses the edge of its container: run over, boil over. In the concealment sense, a cover is laid across a flaw: gloss over, paper over.

The hub is the literal sense. Each spoke is a way of going across or above. All four belong to this unit.

Watch out. Take over is two words; takeover is one, as a verb versus a noun. And run over has three senses: exceed time, review a document quickly, or hit something with a vehicle. The object tells you which.
American / British English. American English uses over freely for more than: "over forty people." Some British style guides prefer more than and reserve over for physical position. This book uses over for more than.

Hold "over means across or covering," and the only question is what goes across: control, attention, a spilling quantity, or a covering laid over a flaw. This is why go over the numbers and gloss over the numbers are near opposites, though they share a particle.
