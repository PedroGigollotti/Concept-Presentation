# EMAIL_STANDARDS.md

**The email standards are not in this repository. They live in Google Drive.**

| Document | Where |
| :---- | :---- |
| EMAIL_STANDARD.md | Google Drive, My Drive root, beside `the-reading-log.md` |
| THE_READING_STANDARD.md | same |
| THE_READING_task.md | same |

They are stored as plain markdown, deliberately not converted to Google Docs.
The conversion is part of how the old duplicate drifted.

## Why they are not here

Cowork reads these standards every week and cannot reach this repository.
A standard lives in exactly one place, and that place is wherever the tool that
reads it can reach.

They used to live in both. The Drive copy fell a version behind and, on
August 24, 2026, Cowork spent a morning working from an EMAIL_STANDARD.md that
still declared no sending platform had been chosen and that two-factor
authentication was off. Both had been resolved on August 12. Keeping two copies
in sync is not the fix; having one copy is.

The full rule is written into the series standard, SERIES_STANDARD.md, section
on where a standard lives.

## Where the book standards are

Not here either. SERIES_STANDARD.md and COHERENCE_CRITERIA.md live in the
Concept kit on the production machine, beside the verifier that enforces them.
This repository holds the published site, the corrections files, and
`THE_READING_design_brief.md`, which records how the visual template was
arrived at rather than governing new work.

## History

The three documents were removed from this repository in the commit that added
this file. Git history keeps them, so nothing is lost, and the history is the
record of where they came from.
