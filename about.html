Pedro Gigollotti

Pedro designed the method. Concept is built on it.

Pedro Gigollotti has taught professional communication for 25 years in six countries: Brazil, the USA, England, Ireland, Malta, and Italy. That range is why Concept can teach how English changes between a London boardroom, a New York sales call, and a Milan negotiation.

Alongside teaching, he works in teacher training, Cambridge examination preparation, and syllabus design. He founded Concept so the method could reach more people, in books and in the Concept Coach app, where the Concept team works with learners between lessons.
