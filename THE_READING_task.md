# The Reading, recurring task

Two tasks. Set up the monthly one as the default. The weekly one is a fallback
for months when the batch did not happen.

---

## TASK A, monthly. Recommended.

**Suggested schedule:** last Thursday of the month, 09:00 Europe/Rome.

**Task prompt, paste this in:**

> Time to plan next month's issues of The Reading, my weekly English newsletter
> for my Bending Spoons students.
>
> Read THE_READING_STANDARD.md and EMAIL_STANDARD.md in the
> Concept-Presentation repo before proposing anything. The visual template is
> locked; only the content changes.
>
> Propose four topics, one per Tuesday next month. For each, give me:
> the expression or structure, which of the five sections it will stretch, the
> register point, which of the four books the pointer goes to, and one line on
> why it is worth a whole issue.
>
> Draw from the content map in section 6 of the standard. Do not repeat a topic
> from a previous issue. Ask me what has come up in lessons before you propose,
> because that is usually better material than anything you can infer.
>
> Text only at this stage. No HTML until I approve the four topics, then the
> drafts.

## TASK B, weekly. Fallback.

**Suggested schedule:** Thursday, 09:00 Europe/Rome.

**Task prompt, paste this in:**

> Draft this week's issue of The Reading. Read THE_READING_STANDARD.md first.
>
> Propose three topics with a line each. I pick one, you draft the text, I
> approve, then you build and load it for a Tuesday 08:00 send.

---

## What to tell me at the start of every session

I do not carry anything between sessions. Give me these four things or I will
guess, and guessing is how a format drifts:

1. **Which issue number** we are producing
2. **What previous issues covered**, briefly, so I do not repeat a topic
3. **Anything from your lessons** worth turning into an issue. This is the best
   material available and I have no access to it
4. **Whether anything in the standards changed** since we last spoke

---

## The production sequence

Fixed. Do not reorder, particularly steps 6 and 7.

1. Topics proposed, you approve one
2. Text drafted against the standard, five sections plus the pointer, under 535
   words to leave room for the pointer line
3. You review the text. Nothing is built before this
4. **Proofread skill run on the text.** Deliberate errors marked protected
   first. A pass returning zero flags is suspicious, not clean
5. Photograph sourced, black and white, 1200px wide, JPEG quality 80, under
   80KB, named `reading-NN-hero.jpg`
6. **Images uploaded to the repo root and verified 200 from outside.** Before
   any HTML is built or loaded
7. HTML built from the locked template, then uploaded to the repo root
8. Second proofread pass on the built HTML
9. Campaign created in MailerLite, custom HTML editor, file loaded by URL
10. Test send to you. Checked on desktop, on phone, and once with images blocked
11. Scheduled for Tuesday 08:00 Europe/Rome
12. You press the final button, not me
