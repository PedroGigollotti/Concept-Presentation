The words from this block

Forty-eight expressions, and every one of them is missing from a sentence the book already wrote.

This is not a test and there is nothing new in it. Each sentence below came out of the unit it belongs to. The expression has been cut out and you put it back. Where the sentence needs a different form of it, write the form the sentence needs, not the form in the list.

C

Over to you

No checking here. This one is yours.

Pick eight of the forty-eight: the eight you would actually use this month, and write one sentence with each, about your own work. Then read them aloud. Any expression you could not put in a real sentence is one you have met and not learned, and it is worth going back for.

Then pick the three you have never used and use them this week, out loud, with a person. That is the only step nobody can do for you.
