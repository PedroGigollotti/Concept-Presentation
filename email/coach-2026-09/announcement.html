Know how your message lands before you send it.

The Concept Coach is an app for professionals who work in English. It helps you choose the right words for the person who will read them, and it teaches you why, so you need it a little less every time. Here is what it does, and why it works differently from the English tools you may already use.

Check a message before you send it

Choose who will read it, paste your message, and see where it sits between casual and formal for that reader. The Coach shows you what to change, and you write the new version yourself. Other tools fix this email. Concept teaches you to write the next one.

Expressions

Type an expression in your language and get it in English, or type an English expression and see what it really means in your language. Each version shows how it sounds to the person reading it. When an expression belongs to British English, the app tells you and gives you the American one, so you always know what you are saying.

Word origins

Type an English word and see where it came from. Once you know why a word works the way it does, you stop guessing.

A guide on every screen

In the corner of every screen there is a small round button with the Concept mark. Tap it and your guide explores what is in front of you, from how people use an expression at work to a quick try of your own.

Two ways to use the Coach

On its own

The Coach is complete by itself. Your plan for the day is built from what you tell us about your work and from what you save. What you save comes back just before you would forget it.

With Concept lessons

If you are a Concept student, the Coach becomes part of your lessons. Your practice for the day comes from your latest lessons and from what you got wrong in them. Your teacher sees what you practice here, so every lesson starts where you are.

Concept is a method, and the Coach is where it meets your working day. On its own, it helps you choose the right words before you send them. With a teacher, it turns every lesson into practice that continues after the lesson ends.

When you open the app, it will ask you a few short questions about your work. It takes about a minute, and your answers decide what the Coach shows you.
