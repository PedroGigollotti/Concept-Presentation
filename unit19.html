19 · THROUGH: traversal, completion, connection, and breakthrough

Getting through it, seeing it through, reaching the other side, and breaking past the barrier.

ACCESS

Read the scene and notice the moves in bold. One of them, power through, is never taught in this book. Before you read on, let the particle show you what it means.

Rosa Two days left. We push through the pipeline review tonight and find the gap.

Ty The Halvorsen deal keeps stalling. I can't get through to their new VP.

Rosa Keep trying. If Halvorsen comes through, we hit the number.

Ty I'll follow through on the pricing they asked for and see the renewal through by Friday.

Rosa Good. We'll power through the forecast tonight, even if it takes until midnight.

Ty And I'll cut through the procurement red tape. Their legal team is the real blocker.

ANALYSIS

Through's hub is physical: from one end to the other. Walk through a door, drive through a tunnel. Every abstract sense in this unit is that passage applied to hardship, a commitment, a connection, or a barrier.

In the traversal sense, you get through something hard and come out the far side: get through, push through. In the completion sense, you carry a thing all the way to its end: see it through, follow through. In the connection sense, you reach the far side and connect: get through to a person, come through. In the breakthrough sense, you pierce a barrier: break through, cut through.

The literal sense sits at the top, and all four senses under it belong to this unit. This particle does not split across two.

Watch out. See through has two senses: complete a project, or perceive the deception behind a claim. And come through is not get through: come through means deliver as promised or arrive; get through means finish or survive.
American / British English. American English uses through for up to and including: Open Monday through Friday. British English says up to Friday or to Friday. This book uses through for inclusive dates.

Hold through means in one side and out the other, and the only question is what you pass through: a hard stretch, a commitment you carry to the end, a line that finally connects, or a barrier you pierce. This is why get through the week and get through to the client differ, though they share a verb and a particle.
