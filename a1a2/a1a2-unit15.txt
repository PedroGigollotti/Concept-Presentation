ANALYSIS
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Goal. Learn to say what is possible. Then learn to say what is not.

THE STRUCTURE
“Can” is not like the other verbs. It never takes an “-s”. The verb after it is always plain: “she can print”. For no, put “not” on the end of it: “can't”. To ask, put it first: “Can you print it?”
   | can + the plain verb  ·  Form: person + can + verb  ·  Meaning: It is possible.  ·  Use it to: say what somebody is able to do  ·  example: She can print it.
   | can't  ·  Form: person + can't + verb  ·  Meaning: It is not possible.  ·  Use it to: say what cannot be done  ·  example: I can't print it.
   | Can, in front  ·  Form: Can + person + verb?  ·  Meaning: You are asking, or you are asking FOR something.  ·  Use it to: ask, and to ask for help  ·  example: Can you help me?

WATCH OUT

   [ No -s, ever. Say “She can print it.” Never say “× She cans print.” Unit 10 put an “-s” on the verb for he and she. “Can” never takes one. No to after can. Say “I can help.” Never say “× I can to help.” The verb after “can” is always plain. You will hardly hear can't. In fast speech “can” is very short and quiet. “Can't” is longer and stronger. If you are not sure, ask: “So you can, or you can't?” Nobody minds. ]

   [ Two ways to say no. “cannot” and “can't” are both correct. “Can't” is what people say. “Cannot” is for when you mean it. Write it as ONE word: “cannot”, never “can not”. ]

THE MOVES
Ready-made lines for asking and answering. Change only the words in blue.
   - Can you [help] me?
   - I can [open] it.
   - I can't [print] it.
   - Yes, I can.
   - No, I can't. Sorry.

WHERE CAN GOES TO ASK
For a question, “can” moves to the front and nothing else changes.
   (figure) No helper is added and no verb changes. The word you already have moves.

WHY THIS WORKS
This is how you ask for anything at work without sounding rude. It is also how you find out whether a thing is possible. Ask it wrong and people still answer you. Miss the “can't” and you stand at a printer. The page is not coming.

A. Can, or can't?

      -> no

      -> yes
   4. (heard, not printed) “She can't drive.”
      -> no
   5. (heard, not printed) “He can't fix the printer.”
      -> no
   6. (heard, not printed) “He can lock the door.”
      -> yes

B. Spot and fix the error
   Each sentence has one mistake. Write it correctly.
   1. × She cans drive.
      -> She can drive.
   2. × I can to help you.
      -> I can help you.
   3. × He can not fixes it.
      -> He cannot fix it.
   4. × Do you can swim?
      -> Can you swim?
   5. × They can't to park here.
      -> They can't park here.

C. Say what is possible
   Write can or can't and the verb in brackets.
   1. The printer is broken. Lena ______ it. (fix)
      -> can't fix
   2. Ana has the key. She ______ the door. (lock)
      -> can lock
   3. The box is too heavy. I ______ it. (lift)
      -> can't lift
   4. Marco has a car. He ______ . (drive)
      -> can drive
   5. The room is full. We ______ here. (park)
      -> can't park
   6. Bruno is free at four. He ______ you. (help)
      -> can help

D. Which is English?
   Look at the verb after it. Choose the English.
   1. ( She can drive / She cans drive ).
      -> She can drive
   2. ( I can help / I can to help ) you.
      -> I can help
   3. ( Can you swim / Do you can swim )?
      -> Can you swim
   4. He ( can't fix / can't fixes ) it.
      -> can't fix
   5. ( Can Marco park / Cans Marco park ) here?
      -> Can Marco park
   6. We ( cannot lift / can not lift ) it.
      -> cannot lift

E. What is it?
   Read the verb. Choose what it means.
   1. lift
      -> b. to raise it off the floor
   2. fix
      -> f. to make it work again
   3. park
      -> d. to leave a car in a place
   4. lock
      -> c. to close it with a key
   5. translate
      -> a. to put it in another language
   6. swim
      -> e. to move through water
