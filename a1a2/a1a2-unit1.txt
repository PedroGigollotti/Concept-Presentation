ANALYSIS
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Goal. Learn the three forms of “to be”: “am”, “is”, “are”. Then say your name, your job, and your country.

THE STRUCTURE
Every English sentence needs a verb. “To be” is the first verb. It has three forms: “am”, “is”, “are”. The word before the verb chooses the form. “I” takes “am”. “He”, “she”, and “it” take “is”. “You”, “we”, and “they” take “are”.
   | I → am  ·  Form: I + am  ·  Meaning: Use “am” only with “I”.  ·  Use it to: talk about yourself  ·  example: I am a nurse.
   | he, she, it → is  ·  Form: he / she / it + is  ·  Meaning: Use “is” for one person or one thing.  ·  Use it to: talk about one other person or thing  ·  example: She is my manager.
   | you, we, they → are  ·  Form: you / we / they + are  ·  Meaning: Use “are” for “you”, and for more than one.  ·  Use it to: talk to someone, or about a group  ·  example: We are a team.

WATCH OUT

   [ Do not drop the verb. Say “I am a nurse,” not “× I a nurse.” Every sentence needs “am”, “is”, or “are”. Match the form to the word before it. Say “He is my manager,” not “× He are my manager.” ]

   [ Short forms. In speech, people say “I'm, you're, he's, she's, it's, we're, they're”. “I am” and “I'm” mean the same thing. Use the short form when you speak. ]

WHERE EACH FORM SITS
   (figure) Three groups, three forms. The word before the verb chooses “am”, “is”, or “are”.

WHY THIS WORKS
You use “am”, “is”, and “are” every day. They are in almost every short sentence about people. With these three words you can say your name. You can say your country. You can say your job. You can say the same about other people.

B. Spot and fix the error
   Each sentence has one mistake. Write it correctly.
   1. × Am a teacher.
      -> I am a teacher.
   2. × She are a nurse.
      -> She is a nurse.
   3. × They is from Spain.
      -> They are from Spain.
   4. × He an engineer.
      -> He is an engineer.
   5. × You is a teacher.
      -> You are a teacher.

C. Write the word
   Ana is a nurse. Marco is a designer. Write he, she, it, we, they, or you.
   1. The nurse is here today. ______ is from Brazil.
      -> she
   2. The designer is here today. ______ is from Italy.
      -> he
   3. The meeting is at nine. ______ is in room four.
      -> it
   4. The nurse and the designer are here. ______ are early.
      -> they
   5. Marco and I are in the office. ______ are on the same team.
      -> we
   6. Ana, ______ are early today.
      -> you

D. Choose the English

   5. You are ( cook / a cook ).
