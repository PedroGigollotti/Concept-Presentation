PREFACE
------------------------------------------------------------------------
This is Book 1 of Professional English Practice, the foundation of the series. It covers levels A1 and A2 in one volume, and takes you from your very first words in English to the point where you can hold a real conversation about your life, your past, your plans, and the world around you.
40 units build the essentials one step at a time: the verb to be, the present, the past, the future, the present perfect, comparison, and the first ways of joining your ideas together. The book grows with you, from simple introductions in Band A1 to fuller, more confident sentences in Band A2. Each unit stands on its own, so you can work from the beginning or open straight to the topic you need this week.
How each unit runs. Every unit follows the same four-part cycle, so once you know the shape, the book never surprises you. Access: listen to a short, real conversation. The grammar you will learn is already there, in bold. Listen before you read: at this level the page cannot tell you how a word sounds, and the recording can. Analysis: learn how the grammar is built, what it means, and how to use it, in plain language with many examples. A simple picture at the end gives the idea a shape, not just a rule. Activation: a full set of exercises, from careful accuracy to real, personal use, ending with a speaking task and a short writing task of your own. Answers: in this interactive edition the key is built into each exercise. Press Check my answers and the correct form appears beside anything you missed, with the recording of the right sentence next to it.
Two bands, one book. Band A1 (Units 1 to 19) builds the essentials. Band A2 (Units 20 to 40) builds fluency, adding the past, the future, the present perfect, comparison, and fuller sentences. A2 reuses everything from A1, so work in order if you are starting out.
Do not skip the last two exercises. Every unit ends with a speaking and writing task of your own, and then with a recording exercise where you say the lines yourself and compare them with the model. That is where the grammar stops being a rule you know and becomes a sentence you actually say.

A WORD FROM THE FOUNDER
------------------------------------------------------------------------
I started The Concept Method because I kept meeting people who wanted to speak English but were stuck right at the start, memorizing lists of rules that never quite turned into real sentences. They knew a lot about English. They just could not yet use it.
This book is built to close that gap from day one. Every conversation in it is something that could happen in your real life, at home or at work, because that is the only place a language actually lives. You will not just learn that the past of go is went. You will use it to tell someone about your weekend.
Go slowly, say everything out loud, and do the speaking tasks even when no one is listening. Grammar becomes fluency through your own mouth, not on the page. Small steps, done often, carry you a very long way.
Mind your future. It is closer than you think, and it begins with your first sentence.
