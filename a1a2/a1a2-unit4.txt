ANALYSIS
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Goal. Learn the six words for “whose”. Then tell two black laptops apart.

THE STRUCTURE
English says “whose” with one small word before the thing. It never changes. “My” is the same for a laptop and for two laptops. It is the same for a man and for a woman. The word follows the OWNER, not the thing. “Her” laptop is a woman's. “His” laptop is a man's.
   | my, your, our  ·  Form: my / your / our + thing  ·  Meaning: Mine, yours, and the group's.  ·  Use it to: claim a thing, or give it back  ·  example: It is my laptop.
   | his, her, its  ·  Form: his / her / its + thing  ·  Meaning: The word follows the OWNER. “Her” laptop is a woman's.  ·  Use it to: say whose, for one other person  ·  example: It is her phone.
   | their  ·  Form: their + thing  ·  Meaning: More than one owner.  ·  Use it to: say whose, for a group  ·  example: It is their room.

WATCH OUT

   [ The word follows the owner, not the thing. Say “her laptop” for a woman. Say “his laptop” for a man. In your language the word follows the THING. This unit exists for that mistake. It never changes. Say “my laptop” and “my laptops.” “My” is the same for one and for more than one. ]

   [ Watch this one. “Its” and “it's” are not the same word. “Its” says whose. “It's” is “it is”. Even people who grew up with English get this wrong. ]

THE MOVES
Ready-made lines for saying whose. Change only the words in blue.
   - It is [my] laptop.
   - [Your] phone is here.
   - Is it [your] bag?
   - It is [her] seat.
   - It is [our] room.

WHO OWNS IT
   (figure) One word before the thing, and it never changes. What changes is the owner.

WHY THIS WORKS
Two black laptops is not a rare problem. It is a Monday. And there is no way out of it without these six words. The two laptops are the same. The only difference is the owner. In your language the word agrees with the THING. In English it agrees with the OWNER. That one difference is most of your mistakes this year.

   5. × It is our the room.

C. Write the word
   Ana is the nurse. Marco is the designer. Write my, your, his, her, our, or their.
   1. The nurse is here. It is ______ laptop.
      -> her
   2. The designer is here. It is ______ bag.
      -> his
   3. The nurse and the designer are here. It is ______ room.
      -> their
   4. I am here. It is ______ seat.
      -> my
   5. You are here. It is ______ coat.
      -> your
   6. Marco and I are here. It is ______ office.
      -> our

D. His or her?
   Look at the owner. Choose the word.

E. What is it?
   Read the thing. Choose what it is.
   1. Marco is here. It is ( her / his ) laptop.
      -> his
   2. Lena is here. It is ( her / his ) bag.
      -> her
   3. Marco is here. ( His / Her ) phone is here.
      -> His
   4. Ana is here. It is ( his / her ) coat.
      -> her
   5. Marco is here. It is ( her / his ) seat.
      -> his
   6. Lena is here. ( Her / His ) room is here.
      -> Her
