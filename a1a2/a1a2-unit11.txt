ANALYSIS
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Goal. Learn to say what somebody does not do. Then learn to ask.

THE STRUCTURE
English does not put “not” on the verb. It puts a helper in front: “do” or “does”. For he, she and it, the helper is “does”. The “-s” moves to the helper. The verb goes back to its plain form. Say “she works”, “she doesn't work”. To ask, put the helper first.
   | don't and doesn't  ·  Form: do not / does not + the plain verb  ·  Meaning: He does not do it.  ·  Use it to: say what somebody does not do  ·  example: She doesn't work on Fridays.
   | Do and Does, in front  ·  Form: Do / Does + person + the plain verb?  ·  Meaning: You are asking.  ·  Use it to: ask what somebody does  ·  example: Does he read the reports?
   | the short answer  ·  Form: Yes, he does. / No, he doesn't.  ·  Meaning: The helper answers for the verb.  ·  Use it to: answer without saying it all again  ·  example: Yes, he does.

WATCH OUT

   [ The -s is on does, not on the verb. Say “Does she work?” Never say “× Does she works?” Unit 10 put the “-s” on the verb. Here it moves to the helper. Do not use no for a verb. Say “He doesn't read them.” Never say “× He no read them.” ]

   [ Two ways to say it. “She does not work” and “She doesn't work” are both correct. The short one is what people say. The long one is for when you mean it. ]

THE MOVES
Ready-made lines for asking. Change only the words in blue.
   - Does [Ana] work on Fridays?
   - She doesn't [work] on Fridays.
   - Do you [need] the file?
   - Yes, I do.
   - No, he doesn't.

WHERE THE -S GOES
The helper takes the -s. The verb goes back to its plain form.
   (figure) The -s leaves the verb and lands on the helper. The verb is plain again.

WHY THIS WORKS
This is how you find out anything about anybody at work. Who does what, and who does not. Ask it wrong and people still answer you. Miss the “doesn't” and you send the file. Nobody is there to open it.

B. Spot and fix the error
   Each sentence has one mistake. Write it correctly.
   1. × Does she works on Fridays?
      -> Does she work on Fridays?
   2. × He no read the reports.
      -> He doesn't read the reports.
   3. × Do she answer the phone?
      -> Does she answer the phone?
   4. × She doesn't checks the email.
      -> She doesn't check the email.
   5. × He not opens the office.
      -> He doesn't open the office.

C. Say what they do not do
   Write don't or doesn't.
   1. Ana ______ work on Fridays.
      -> doesn't
   2. I ______ need the file today.
      -> don't
   3. Marco ______ answer the phone.
      -> doesn't
   4. We ______ use the small room.
      -> don't
   5. She ______ bring the coffee.
      -> doesn't
   6. They ______ open the office.
      -> don't

D. Do or does?
   Look at the word after the gap. Choose the English.
   1. ( Does she work / Does she works ) on Fridays?
      -> Does she work
   2. ( Do you need / Does you need ) the file?
      -> Do you need
   3. He ( doesn't read / doesn't reads ) them.
      -> doesn't read
   4. ( Does Marco bring / Do Marco bring ) the coffee?
      -> Does Marco bring
   5. They ( don't use / doesn't use ) the small room.
      -> don't use
   6. ( Does she know / Do she know ) the answer?
      -> Does she know

E. What is it?
   Read the verb. Choose what it means.
   1. send
      -> c. to make it go to somebody
   2. answer
      -> d. to say something back
   3. check
      -> b. to look at it and see if it is right
   4. bring
      -> e. to carry it to somebody
   5. need
      -> f. to have to have it
   6. know
      -> a. to have it in your head already
   the endings as the student sees them:
      a. to have it in your head already
      b. to look at it and see if it is right
      c. to make it go to somebody
      d. to say something back
      e. to carry it to somebody
      f. to have to have it
