ANALYSIS

Goal. Learn the six words for whose. Then tell two black laptops apart.

The structure

English says whose with one small word before the thing. It never changes. My is the same for a laptop and for two laptops. It is the same for a man and for a woman. The word follows the OWNER, not the thing. Her laptop is a woman's. His laptop is a man's.

my, your, our

Form
my / your / our + thing
Meaning
Mine, yours, and the group's.
Use it to
claim a thing, or give it back
It is my laptop.

his, her, its

Form
his / her / its + thing
Meaning
The word follows the OWNER. Her laptop is a woman's.
Use it to
say whose, for one other person
It is her phone.

their

Form
their + thing
Meaning
More than one owner.
Use it to
say whose, for a group
It is their room.

Watch out

The word follows the owner, not the thing. Say “her laptop” for a woman. Say “his laptop” for a man. In your language the word follows the THING. This unit exists for that mistake.

It never changes. Say “my laptop” and “my laptops.” My is the same for one and for more than one.

Watch this one. Its and it's are not the same word. Its says whose. It's is it is. Even people who grew up with English get this wrong.

The moves

Ready-made lines for saying whose. Change only the words in blue.

  • It is [my] laptop.
  • [Your] phone is here.
  • Is it [your] bag?
  • It is [her] seat.
  • It is [our] room.

Why this works

Two black laptops is not a rare problem. It is a Monday. And there is no way out of it without these six words. The two laptops are the same. The only difference is the owner. In your language the word agrees with the THING. In English it agrees with the OWNER. That one difference is most of your mistakes this year.
