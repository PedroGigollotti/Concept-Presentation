ANALYSIS
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Goal. Learn to say what a room has. Then say what it does not have.

THE STRUCTURE
English puts two words in front: “there is” or “there are”. The choice comes from the thing AFTER it, never from the room. One thing takes “there is”. More than one takes “there are”. Water and coffee take “there is”: there is no number in them.
   | there is (one thing)  ·  Form: there is + one thing  ·  Meaning: The room has it.  ·  Use it to: name one thing in a room  ·  example: There is a board.
   | there are (more than one)  ·  Form: there are + more than one  ·  Meaning: The room has several.  ·  Use it to: count what is there  ·  example: There are chairs.
   | there are no (nothing, more than one)  ·  Form: there are no + more than one  ·  Meaning: The room has none of them.  ·  Use it to: say the room has none of them  ·  example: There are no cups.
   | there is no (nothing)  ·  Form: there is no + the thing  ·  Meaning: The room does not have it.  ·  Use it to: say what a room does not have  ·  example: There is no screen.

WATCH OUT

   [ Match is or are to the thing after it. Say “There is a chair” for one. Say “There are chairs” for more. Never say “× There is two chairs.” This unit exists for that mistake. Water and coffee take is. Water has no number. Say “There is water,” never “× There are waters.” ]

   [ Two ways to say it. “There is no water” and “There isn't any water” are both correct. Both are ordinary. The first is shorter. The second is the one people say more often. ]

THE MOVES
Ready-made lines for a room. Change only the words in blue.
   - There is [a board].
   - There are [chairs].
   - There is no [screen].
   - Is there [water]?
   - There is [coffee].

WHY THIS WORKS
In your language one word does this and it never changes. English makes you look at what comes next. Say it wrong and nobody stops you. Say “there is no screen” and somebody brings one. That is the reason to learn it.
