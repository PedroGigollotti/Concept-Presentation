ANALYSIS
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Goal. Learn “a”, “an”, and the plural “-s”. Then name your own things.

THE STRUCTURE
One thing takes a small word in front: “a” or “an”. Your EAR chooses, not your eye. Use “an” before a vowel SOUND. Use “a” before a consonant SOUND. So it is “an hour” and “a hotel”. More than one takes “-s”. Then “a” and “an” go away. Some plurals do not add “-s”. Learn the pairs one by one.
   | a + consonant sound  ·  Form: a + one thing  ·  Meaning: One thing. The next sound is a consonant.  ·  Use it to: name one thing  ·  example: It is a box.
   | an + vowel sound  ·  Form: an + one thing  ·  Meaning: One thing. The next sound is a, e, i, o or u.  ·  Use it to: name one thing  ·  example: It is an apple.
   | the plural -s  ·  Form: the thing + s, and no a or an  ·  Meaning: More than one.  ·  Use it to: name more than one  ·  example: They are copies.

WATCH OUT

   [ It is the SOUND, not the letter. “Hour” begins with the letter h. The SOUND is a vowel. So it is “an hour,” never “× a hour.” “University” begins with a vowel letter. The SOUND is a consonant. So it is “a university.” Say the word out loud. The first sound decides. No “a” or “an” with a plural. Say “They are boxes,” not “× They are a boxes.” “A” and “an” mean one. Three spellings, and the bank shows all three. Add “-s” to window. Add “-es” after x, ch and s: box, boxes. Change y to “-ies”: copy, copies. ]

   [ Watch these. Some plurals do not add “-s” at all. Learn each pair. Man and men. Woman and women. Person and people. Foot and feet. Tooth and teeth. ]

THE MOVES
Ready-made lines for naming things. Change only the words in blue.
   - It is [a] window.
   - It is [an] apple.
   - They are [copies].
   - It is not [a] box.
   - Is it [an] envelope?

WHY THIS WORKS
In your language a thing is often alone. It is box. In English it is never alone. It is “a” box. Or it is “boxes”. And nothing else. Say it with no small word. It is wrong at once. It is not a rule for your head. It is a habit for your ear. Say the words aloud. Soon it is easy.
