20 · AWAY: removal, departure, disappearance, and persistence

Getting rid of it, leaving, letting it vanish, and keeping at it.

ACCESS

Read the scene and notice the moves in bold. One of them, square away, is never taught in this book. Before you read on, let the particle show you what it means.

A people-operations team facing rising attrition in the months after a reorg.

Dana Attrition's up. Three strong engineers walked away last month.

Ravi And we can't just explain away the exit-survey scores. The complaints are consistent.

Dana Agreed. Let's not throw away the flexible-hours pilot. That's the one thing people liked.

Dana Right. Let's square away the retention offers before Friday, so no one else turns down a counter.

Ravi I'll plug away at the manager training. Morale won't fade away on its own.

ANALYSIS

Away's hub is physical: to a distance. Walk away, move it away, put it away. Every abstract sense in this unit is that distance applied to removal, departure, vanishing, or steady work out of the spotlight.

In the removal sense, you send something off for good: give away, throw away. In the departure sense, a person moves off: walk away, turn away. In the disappearance sense, something dwindles until nothing is left: fade away, explain away. In the persistence sense, you keep an action going steadily: chip away, plug away.

The literal sense sits at the top, and all four senses under it belong to this unit. This particle does not split across two.

Watch out. Give away has two senses: donate a prize, or accidentally reveal a secret. And explain away is not explain: explain away means dismiss something with a convenient reason, often to avoid dealing with it.
American / British English. British English says straightaway or right away for immediately. American English uses right away and rarely straightaway. This book uses right away.

Hold away means off to a distance, and the only question is what goes: something discarded for good, a person who leaves, a signal that dwindles to nothing, or an effort kept going quietly at a remove. This is why walk away sounds like giving up and chip away sounds like sticking with it, though they share a particle.
