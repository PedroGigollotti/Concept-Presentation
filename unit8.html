8 · OUT: visibility, distribution, and thoroughness

Discovering things, announcing them, and getting them done.

ACCESS

Read the scene and notice the moves in bold. One of them, spell out, you may not have met. Before you read on, let the particle show you what it means.

A national retailer's analytics team, the night before they present to the regional directors.

Dana We finally found out why returns spiked in March. It wasn't the packaging.

Marco So the fix worked out in the two test stores. Do we roll out the change to every store at once, or stage it?

Layla Let's spell out the plan before we point out anything to the directors.

Dana Agreed. But sort out the regional numbers first. Two of them still don't turn out clean.

Marco I'll hand out the draft deck tonight so everyone reads it. And we hear the store managers out before the meeting, right?

Dana Yes. Send the agenda out by nine. If the Midwest turns out the way I expect, we point out the vendor, not the staff.

ANALYSIS

Out is the same hub you met in Unit 7: away from an enclosure. That unit used it for things leaving or being removed. This one takes the other direction of the same movement, when what comes out of the enclosure is meant to be seen, spread, or carried all the way through.

In the visibility sense, information comes out of hiding into view: you find out a cause, point out a risk. In the distribution sense, something moves outward from one center to many: you hand out a deck, roll out a change. In the thoroughness sense, out marks a job taken to the end: you sort out a mess, hear someone out before you answer.

Nothing about the particle changed since Unit 7. Only which half of its map this unit works on.

Watch out. Point out sends the fact to a person with to: “point out the risk to the client,” not point out the client the risk. And hear out wraps its object: let me hear you out, never hear out you.
American / British English. To circulate a document to a group, Americans send it out; the British often send it round. This book uses send out.

Hold out means into the open, and the only question left is what comes out. A fact comes into view, a delivery goes out to many, or a job gets carried all the way through. Visibility, distribution, thoroughness: three things emerging from the same enclosure, and the verb tells you which one.

thrash out: to argue through a problem thoroughly until it is resolved. Thoroughness sense.

put out: to issue or release something to the public. Distribution sense.

single out: to pick one thing from a group and make it the one everyone sees. Visibility sense.
