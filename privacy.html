2. What we collect

2.1 On the website

  • The website has one form, Talk to us. It sends your name, your email address, your company if you give it, and your message to contact@theconceptmethod.com through Resend, our email service, and to limit misuse it counts messages per IP address for one hour. The website keeps nothing. It has no analytics and no advertising code.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the website, and the fonts come from Google Fonts. Your browser connects to both, so they receive your IP address and information about your browser.
  • A locked book page asks Concept Lessons whether your browser is already signed in. If you choose "Enter with my email," you go to the Concept Lessons sign-in page, and you type your email address there, not on the website.
  • Your answers in the book exercises are not sent anywhere and are not saved.
  • If you write to contact@theconceptmethod.com, your message is kept in our email account.

2.2 In Concept Lessons

Your registration

  • Your name, email address, level, teacher, and class. If a company pays for your lessons, the name of that company.
  • What you told us when you joined: your role, your field, what you need English for, where it goes wrong, who you write to, and a short text about you of up to 800 characters.
  • Your teacher's notes about you and about your lessons, and the class diary.

Your work

  • Every answer you give in a lesson, with your attempts, your scores, and the time you spent.
  • What you type in an answer box. It is sent to the server a few seconds after you stop typing, before you press Check, so that your teacher can follow your work in class.
  • Your recordings for spoken tasks, up to 8 MB each.
  • The personal task written for you ("In your words").
  • Your lines on the class whiteboard. A wrong answer of yours can be used for a "Correct it" line.
  • Your reviews, the answers your teacher accepted, and how many written checks you used each day.

Your lessons and billing

  • Your lesson times, read from our lesson calendar: your name as written in the calendar, your email address as a guest, the start time, the length, and whether the lesson was held, moved, or canceled, with the notice given.
  • Reports of your hours, with diary entries your teacher chose, when a report is sent to you or to the company that pays.
  • For a company client: the company name, the address for reports, the contracts, and the hours bought.

Signing in

  • A key for each browser you sign in with.
  • Invitation links, and email sign-in links and codes, with your email address.
  • The IP address of each sign-in attempt, to limit the number of attempts.

If you were invited to try the Coach: your name, your email address, the dates of your trial, and what you told us when you joined.

2.3 In the Concept Coach app

The app does not store your name or your email address. It knows you by the key that Concept Lessons gives you. When the app shows your name, it asks Concept Lessons for it and does not keep it.

  • Every exchange with a coach: your question as you typed it, the answer, the screen, book, and unit you were on, and what the answer cost.
  • Your result in each practice exercise.
  • "Write it now": the original sentence, your earlier answer, your new attempt, and the coach's feedback.
  • Your Find my level result: one book to start with and two or three lines that explain it. There is no score.
  • Which units you opened, and when.
  • Problems you report, in your words, with the screen and the app version.
  • Your Notebook: the words you saved, their review dates, and the words you looked up.
  • Your reminder settings, and the address your phone gives the app for notifications.
  • Your birthday, if you give it: the day and the month only. The app never asks for the year.
  • Which expressions of the day you were shown.
  • Your permission to send your words to the services in section 4, and which version of the text you agreed to.
  • Sign-in attempts: the email address typed and your IP address.

Your voice. When you hold the microphone button, your recording is sent to ElevenLabs to be turned into text. The app does not keep the recording.

The app collects no advertising identifier, no location, no contacts, and no photos. It has no analytics.

Since October 16, 2026, the app sends none of your words and none of your recordings to the services in section 4 until you give permission in the app.

5. Who can see it at Concept

  • You see your own lesson pages, your record, your board, your notes, your personal task, and your reviews.
  • Your teacher sees your registration, your answers (including what you type before you press Check), your recordings, your board, the notes, the diary, and your reports.
  • From the Coach, your teacher receives two things. The first is a Register Check, which is sent only when you choose to share it. It holds the text you checked (up to 2,000 characters), the screen, and the reader you chose. The sentence beside the button says that your teacher sees it. The second is a written attempt from "Write it now," with the original sentence and the coach's feedback.
  • A question you ask in the Coach panel is never sent to your teacher.
  • If you change teacher, your history goes with you to the new teacher.
  • The director (Pedro Gigollotti, who runs The Concept Method) can see every learner's record. He also owns the accounts that hold the databases, and he can open them to keep the service working.
  • If you are an individual client, the director sees how often you use each part of the Coach, how long answers take, and what they cost. These counts never include your words. If you are trying the app, what you send with Send feedback reaches him with the name of the screen.
  • A company that pays for your lessons has no login and no page. Your teacher or the director can email a report to the company's contact: your name, your hours, and diary entries they chose. This is never done for individual clients.

7. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • get a copy of the data we hold about you;
  • have wrong data corrected;
  • have your data deleted;
  • have its use limited;
  • object to a use based on legitimate interest;
  • receive the data you gave us in a format a computer can read, and send it to someone else;
  • withdraw your consent at any time, where a use is based on consent. This does not change what was done before.

If the law where you live gives you more rights than the GDPR, for example Brazil's data protection law (LGPD), Concept gives you those rights too.

How to use them. Write to contact@theconceptmethod.com. We answer within one month. If a request is complex, we can take two more months, and we tell you within the first month.

A copy of your data. There is no download button yet. When you ask, we prepare the copy by hand from our databases and send it to you.

Deleting your Coach data. In the app, open the menu and choose Delete my account. The app lists what it holds about you, asks you to confirm twice, deletes it at once, and shows you what it deleted. It also clears the app's copy on your phone and your notification address.

This does not delete your Concept Lessons registration. To delete it, write to us. When we remove you, we delete what section 6 lists. We keep your lesson times, the reports already sent, and the contracts, because billing needs them.

In both cases, the backups keep the data for up to 8 days (section 6).

8. How to complain

If you are not happy with our answer, you can complain to a data protection authority. In Italy this is the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali, Piazza Venezia 11, 00187 Roma, www.garanteprivacy.it.

You can also complain to the authority in the EU country where you live or work, or where you think the problem happened.

9. Cookies and storage on your device

We use cookies only to keep you signed in. We use no cookies for analytics or advertising.

concept_key

Keeps you signed in on Concept Lessons, the Coach, and the books. It lasts one year (one hour for a demo session).

concept_alt

A second sign-in key, for a teacher who is also a learner. It lasts one year.

concept_view

Concept Lessons, for the director only: remembers the "view all" setting for one year.

cc_unlocked

The Coach: remembers that you passed its door. It lasts one day.

cc_preview

The Coach: used only with a preview link. It ends when the link ends.

Your browser also keeps some data in its own storage:

  • Concept Lessons: a copy of your lesson record, the answers you have not checked yet, the answers waiting to be sent, and small marks such as a finished step or the whiteboard's position.
  • The Coach: a copy of your Notebook, your conversation with the coach, your choices (such as the reader and whether sound is on), your look-up history, and small marks of what you have seen. Delete my account clears all of it.
  • The website: the language you chose in the bar (concept_lang), a mark that the books are open on this device (cm_access, cm_pv_access), and a mark that you closed the privacy message (cm_privacy, cm_privacy_seen, cm_pv_privacy_seen).

You can delete these at any time in your browser settings. You will then need to sign in again.

When what we collect, who receives it, or how long we keep it changes, we update this notice and the date at the top.
